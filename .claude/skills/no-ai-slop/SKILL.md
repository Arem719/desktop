# No AI slop

## Banned patterns (rewrite on sight)
- Filler openers/closers: "In today's fast-paced world", "Great question!", "I hope this helps", "Let's dive in", "In conclusion".
- Inflated vocabulary: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, unlock, elevate, tapestry, realm, landscape, game-changer, cutting-edge, harness, navigate (figuratively), testament.
- "It's not just X, it's Y" constructions; rhetorical triplets everywhere ("fast, simple, and powerful").
- Em-dash overuse; emoji bullets; every paragraph ending in a neat moral.
- Vague claims with no number, name or example.
- Hedging stacks: "may potentially help to somewhat improve".

## Rules
1. Say the concrete thing: numbers, names, specific examples, the actual command.
2. Vary sentence length. Short sentences are fine.
3. Cut any sentence that survives deletion without loss of meaning.
4. Match the user's voice: if they gave samples, mirror their vocabulary and punctuation.
5. Prefer active voice and plain verbs ("use", "make", "fix").

## Process
Draft → scan against the banned list → cut 20–30% → read aloud test (would a person say this?) → deliver. When rewriting someone's text, show the result first, then (optional, one line) what changed.
