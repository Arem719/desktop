# Idea verdict: do / clarify / drop

Score each 1–5 with a one-line reason:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Problem | Is the pain real, frequent, and felt by a specific person? |
| Demand evidence | Are people already paying, hacking workarounds, or searching for it? |
| Alternatives | What do they use today, and why would they switch? |
| Reach | Can the user actually reach these customers (channel)? |
| Feasibility | Can it be built by this user with their skills/time/budget? MVP in ≤ 2 weeks? |
| Money | Who pays, how much, and does the math work? |
| Unfair advantage | Why this user, why now? |
| Risk | Legal, platform-dependency, or tech blockers? |

## Verdict rules
- **DO IT** – total ≥ 28 and no criterion is 1. Give the smallest MVP and the first 3 steps.
- **CLARIFY** – promising but ≥ 1 unknown decides it. List the exact questions / a cheap test (landing page, 10 user interviews, pre-sale) to answer them in ≤ 1 week.
- **DROP IT** – any fatal 1 or total < 20. Say why plainly, then suggest one pivot that fixes the weakest point.

Be direct; no cheerleading. Verdict in the first line.
